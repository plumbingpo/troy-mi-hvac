Homeowner maintenance is real but short: filters changed on schedule, the outdoor coil kept clear, registers open, drains glanced at. Everything else requires instruments, because the failures that strand you do not announce themselves visually. A capacitor at 80 percent of rating looks exactly like a healthy one; only a meter can tell. A charge that drifted low shows up only in superheat and subcooling. Maintenance Tips for HVAC Maintenance

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Put these technician-approved steps to work around your property:

  • Leave every supply and return register open, including those in unused rooms
  • Clear the condensate drain early in cooling season, before algae has a chance to grow
  • Give the condenser room to breathe and rinse its coil lightly each spring
  • Check carbon monoxide alarms monthly in any home with fuel-burning appliances
  • Replace your filter on a one- to three-month cycle; nothing else you do pays off more
  • Schedule maintenance in early spring and early fall, ahead of the busy season and the extreme weather
  • Pay attention to new noises or longer cycles and mention them at your next visit
