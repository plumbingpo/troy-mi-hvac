Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Ann Arbor

Many mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, central air usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of summer.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored through a full season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so you can reach us fast as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
